        How the numbers work

        Current values are an average across free dynasty value sites, each measuring something different:

        • KeepTradeCut: crowdsourced keep/trade/cut votes.
        • FantasyCalc: real trades made in synced leagues.
        • DynastyProcess: FantasyPros expert consensus rankings turned into values.
        • RotoWire: a monthly expert trade value chart (Superflex only).
        • FantasyPros Trade Value Chart: a monthly expert chart, added by the daily updater when it can read it.

        Every site uses its own scale and curve, so each is mapped onto DynastyProcess’s scale by rank before averaging: a site’s 10th-most-valuable player gets DynastyProcess’s 10th-highest value, and players and picks in between are interpolated. Each site’s ordering is kept; only the numbers are made comparable. A player is averaged over the sites that list him; open any player in Rankings to see each site’s number, the average, the median and the spread. Switch to the median or a single site under League format.

        History charts, week-over-week changes, market signals and past weeks use DynastyProcess alone, because it’s the only one with free weekly archives (Aug 8, 2025 onward, with no snapshots between Feb 27 and Apr 30, 2026).

        • Superflex uses each site’s separate Superflex/2QB values, so quarterbacks are priced by the data itself.
        • PPR and TE premium are adjustments layered on top, because the sites don’t provide them. Full PPR is the baseline. Each position's value is multiplied as shown below.
        • Draft picks: 2026 slots (still listed in the source, though most rookie drafts are done by now), plus 2027 and 2028 values from the source. Early, mid and late 2028 picks and every 2029 pick are estimates, marked "est.", projected from how far-out picks have lost value in each week's data.
        • Roster fit (after you import a Sleeper league): each team's best lineup is filled from your league's real starting slots. A player's fit value is what he adds to the roster receiving him: starters add close to full value. Bench players earn a share of it (20%, 40% or 60%, your choice), with diminishing credit for deeper backups at the same position (1st backup 100%, 2nd 70%, 3rd 45%, 4th and beyond 25%). So a third quarterback on a team that already has two is worth much less than a first backup. Draft picks always count at market value, and the depth discount is off in this mode. Each team’s picks come from Sleeper (own picks plus any traded picks), with early, mid or late projected from that team’s lineup strength, so weaker teams’ picks are worth more.
        • Commentary: the one-liner under the grade is picked at random from a pool for that grade band, so the same trade can get a different take (tap Another take). Choose Off, Clean or Spicy in the settings.
        • Skinky grade: every trade gets a score from 0 (skunkiest) to 100 (skinkiest) from your side of the table, shown on the sliding scale. It blends how the value balances (what you get against what you send) with what the trade does to your roster when a league is imported. Beyond a 35% swing it maxes out. Their side gets its own grade in the roster-fit panel.
        • Team modes: each team starts in a mode inferred from roster strength alone (top third of the league by lineup value Contend, bottom third Rebuild, the rest Balanced; win-loss records aren’t used), and you can override any team. The mode changes how a team values what it receives, and only applies in Roster-fit mode. Contend: young players (adjusted age 23 or under) ×0.75, picks ×0.75, veterans (adjusted age 27 or older) ×1.05, starting-lineup upgrades ×1.10 and bench depth ×0.85. Rebuild: young QB ×1.30, WR ×1.15, TE ×1.15 (adjusted age 23 or under); adjusted age 24 to 25 ×1.05, 26 ×1.00, 27 ×0.90, 28 ×0.80, older ×0.65; picks ×1.15; lineup upgrades ×0.90 and bench depth ×1.20. Running backs get their own, steeper curve for rebuilding teams by real age: 22 and under ×1.00, 23 ×0.90, 24 ×0.80, 25 ×0.68, 26 ×0.55, 27 and older ×0.40. Adjusted age shifts by position first: quarterbacks count 3 years younger, tight ends 1 year younger, running backs 1.5 years older, wide receivers unchanged.
        • Market signals: a player is flagged Sell high after a 12% or larger rise in four weeks, or when he’s at his peak value past his prime age. Buy low means a 12% or larger drop in four weeks, or a young player far below his peak. Rough screens, not predictions.
        • Trade finder: tries 1-for-1, 2-for-1, 1-for-2 and 2-for-2 packages from each roster’s top assets, keeps ones within 12% of market value where both rosters gain, and ranks them by your gain.
        • Depth discount: on each side, assets are sorted high to low, and the 2nd counts 92%, the 3rd 84%, and so on down to a 60% floor.
        • Values don't reflect injuries or news after each snapshot. Use it as a starting point for a negotiation, not a verdict.

        Your leagues, settings and current trade are saved, so they’re still here when you reopen this page: privately to your account when opened inside Claude, otherwise in this browser on this device.
